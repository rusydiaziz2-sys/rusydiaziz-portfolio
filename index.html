Final year project

Computer Vision-Based Mosque Smart Lighting

Team project (4 people)
Mar 2025 – Jan 2026
Role
Electrical system & programming lead
Stack
Razer Kiyo Pro, Raspberry Pi 4, YOLO (Ultralytics), OpenCV, NumPy, ESP32, UART/USB serial
Approach
Raspberry Pi 4 runs YOLO on live camera feed to detect occupant zones, then sends zone-condition control signals over UART to an ESP32, which drives the electrical system to adjust lighting intensity per zone (9-zone adaptive dimming, tested across single- and multi-occupant scenarios).
  • 25% lower electricity use
  • 9-zone real-time detection
  • Fully functional prototype
